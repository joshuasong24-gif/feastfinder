Privacy Policy

Feast Finder Deals · Effective September 25, 2026

Feast Finder Deals ("FeastFinder", "we", "us") is an iPhone app made by Joshua Song that helps you find restaurant deals. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it.

The short version: you can browse deals without an account. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If you sign in, we store your account and the deals and restaurants you save so they sync across your devices. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Information we use

WhatWhenWhy
Name, email address and a user ID from Sign in with Apple or GoogleOnly if you sign inTo create your account and let you sign back in. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we receive a relay address instead of your real email.
Saved deals, favorite restaurants, deals you've marked as used, and reminder settingsOnly if you sign inTo sync your saves and reminders across devices.
Your locationOnly if you allow location accessTo choose deals for your city. Your location is used on your iPhone and with Apple's map and address services. The app does not send your location to our servers.

Technical request data, such as IP addressWhen the app downloads the deal listThe deal list is a public file hosted on GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub may log your IP address and device information when the app downloads it. That download contains no account or location information.

Who else handles your information

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and App Check) stores your account and synced saves. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's Firebase terms. App Check uses Apple's App Attest to confirm requests come from the real app.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, location, maps and notifications under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google handles Google Sign-In under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub hosts the public deal list under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Restaurant links

Deals link to restaurant websites and apps. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we don't control what they collect. FeastFinder isn't affiliated with the restaurants it lists.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and synced saves while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account record and synced saves from Firebase. Some copies may remain for a limited time in the service provider's backups or logs before they're removed on its normal schedule.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your account and synced data. It also revokes Sign in with Apple, if you used it.
  • Location: turn it off at any time in iPhone Settings. You can still browse by choosing a city or seeing chain-wide deals.
  • Notifications: turn them off in iPhone Settings or in the app.
  • Access or other requests: email us at the address below.

Children

FeastFinder isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Some listings include happy-hour drink offers intended for adults of legal drinking age.

Security

We use access rules that limit each account's data to its owner, encrypted connections, and Apple App Attest. No system is perfectly secure. To report a security problem, email us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app.
