Privacy Policy

Feast Finder Deals Support

Questions, a wrong or expired deal, or an account problem? Email feastfinderdeals@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

A deal didn't work at the restaurant

Restaurants set their own offers and can change them or end them early. Always check the linked source and the restaurant's terms before you order. Please email us the deal name and location so we can fix or remove it.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse without one. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want to save deals, favorite restaurants and keep reminders across devices.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account and synced data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why am I not seeing local deals?

FeastFinder currently covers places in the United States. Allow location access or choose a city in Settings. Without a city, you'll still see chain-wide deals.

Reminders aren't showing up

Make sure notifications are allowed for FeastFinder in iPhone Settings, then Notifications.
